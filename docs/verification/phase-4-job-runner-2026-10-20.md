# Phase 4 private job-runner checkpoint — 2026-10-20

The inactive job runner joins the private encrypted SQLite store, startup recovery gate and one-request native systemd unit. It serializes recovery before claiming a job, keeps one claim through unit cleanup and finalization, and will not start a second claim while the first is active. A completed result is persisted only after the unit has no remaining main PID. An error with verified unit cleanup becomes a bounded claim-bound failure immediately. If cleanup cannot be confirmed, the claim remains running and the next step must repeat unit recovery before another claim. A job changed to `cancelling` cannot be completed as successful output.

Mock RED→GREEN tests cover recovery/claim ordering, serial launches, confirmed and unconfirmed failures, and cancellation finalization. A separate root-only Debian 13 proof used real transient units and a disposable encrypted store: a synthetic multilingual TXT job completed and reopened, while an unsupported OCR request failed with its specific safe error after confirmed cleanup. No document-job unit remained active and the enrolled gateway PID stayed unchanged. Focused tests passed21/21, full Windows228plus3POSIX skips and Debian231/231 passed, and independent review found no high-priority issue. The source is not deployed.

This candidate is not a persistent single-instance service. It does not yet accept authenticated gateway requests, abort an active worker on external cancellation, bind every job to a durable unit identity across a supervisor crash, or coordinate update/rollback and full joint-load qualification. These are required Phase 4 gates.
